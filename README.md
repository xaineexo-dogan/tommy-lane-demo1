# Catalog Connect

Web-App Portal Strategy & Project Proposal

Prepared For

Thomas Lane
Tommy Lane Publishing LLC

1. Project Overview

Tommy Lane Publishing has spent more than four decades building a catalog of professionally recorded music with clear ownership of both master and publishing rights.

The next step is presenting that catalog in a way that reflects its value and makes it accessible to music supervisors, production companies, advertising agencies, and other licensing professionals.

This project focuses on creating a custom website that serves as both a marketing tool and a business platform. Beyond showcasing the music, the website will establish credibility, simplify the licensing process, and provide potential clients with an organized way to explore the catalog and submit inquiries.

The site will be designed to grow alongside the business, making it easy to expand the catalog, publish news, and add future licensing opportunities without requiring a complete redesign.

2. Project Goals

The primary objectives of this project are to:

Present Tommy Lane Publishing as a professional and trustworthy music publishing company.

Showcase the catalog in a way that encourages exploration and discovery.

Make it easy for licensing professionals to review songs and request additional information.

Build confidence by highlighting the company's history, ownership rights, and industry experience.

Create a modern, responsive website that performs well across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

The website should leave visitors with the impression that they are working with an established publisher whose music is organized, legally prepared, and ready for licensing opportunities.

4. Homepage

The homepage will introduce visitors to Tommy Lane Publishing and immediately communicate what the company offers.

Rather than overwhelming visitors with information, the homepage will guide them toward exploring the catalog or contacting the company for licensing opportunities.

4.1 Hero Section

A clean introductory section featuring a strong headline supported by concise messaging about the company's music catalog and licensing availability. Suggested messaging will emphasize:

45 years of professional music experience

Ownership of master and publishing rights

Professionally recorded catalog

Music available for film, television, advertising, and digital media

Primary Calls to Action

Browse the Music Catalog

Contact for Licensing

4.2 Featured Music

The homepage will introduce several featured songs representing the strongest genres within the catalog.

Each featured track will display:

Album artwork

Song title

Artist name

Genre

A short preview clip

Link to licensing inquiry

This section should rotate over time to highlight different artists and seasonal content.

4.3 Genre Overview

Visitors should be able to immediately understand the diversity of the catalog. Genres will be presented visually, allowing users to browse music by category. Each genre will link directly to its dedicated collection.

4.4 Company Introduction

A short section introducing Tommy Lane Publishing and its history will reinforce credibility without requiring visitors to leave the homepage.

This Section Will Highlight

Years in business

BMI affiliation

Professional recordings

Rights ownership

Commitment to music licensing

Visitors interested in learning more can continue to the full About page.

4.5 Licensing Call-to-Action

Because sync licensing is the primary business objective, the homepage will include a dedicated section for production companies, music supervisors, advertising agencies, and other licensing professionals.

This section will explain that the catalog is professionally recorded, organized, and available for licensing inquiries.

A clear call-to-action will direct visitors to the contact page or licensing request form.

8. Design Direction

The website should build upon the existing Tommy Lane Publishing branding while introducing a cleaner and more modern visual style.

The current flyer and promotional materials will serve as the foundation for the design.

Rather than replacing the existing identity, the design will refine it by improving typography, layout, color consistency, and overall presentation for digital use.

The finished website should feel timeless, approachable, and professional while remaining authentic to the company's history and musical heritage.

9. User Experience

The website should be simple to navigate for both casual visitors and industry professionals.

Key Priorities

Fast page loading

Mobile-friendly layouts

Clear navigation

Easy access to music previews

Straightforward licensing inquiries

Consistent design throughout the site

Every page should encourage visitors to continue exploring the catalog or initiate contact without unnecessary complexity.

10. Licensing Portal

One of the primary goals of this website is to create a professional experience for music supervisors, production companies, advertising agencies, filmmakers, and other licensing professionals.

To support this, the website will include a secure area where approved users can access additional catalog resources not available to the general public.

Access will be granted through a request form where professionals can provide their company information and describe the nature of their projects. Once approved, users will receive login credentials to access the protected section.

The Licensing Portal May Include

High-quality audio downloads

Song metadata and technical information

Copyright and publishing documentation (where appropriate)

Licensing request forms

Contact information for direct communication

The objective is to simplify the review process while presenting Tommy Lane Publishing as an organized and reliable licensing partner.

13. Search Engine Optimization

As part of the website development process, a standard SEO plugin will be installed and configured to establish a basic SEO foundation. This includes configuring essential settings, generating XML sitemaps, and enabling page title and meta description management.

This setup provides the tools needed for basic on-page SEO and does not include a comprehensive SEO strategy or ongoing optimization services.
